The Story

WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL?

In Japanese cuisine, flavor is often built not only with salt or fat, but with umami. Kombu is naturally rich in glutamate, while dried shiitake mushrooms are rich in guanylate. Layered with the savory richness of beef and pork, the result is deeper, rounder and more satisfying.

ITALIAN TRADITION. JAPANESE UMAMI. ONE UNFORGETTABLE BOLOGNESE.

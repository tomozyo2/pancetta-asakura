The Story

WHAT MAKES SAMURAI BOLOGNESE SPECIAL?

In Japanese cuisine, flavor is often built not only with salt or fat, but with umami. Kombu is naturally rich in glutamate, while dried shiitake mushrooms are rich in guanylate. When these are layered with the savory richness of beef and pork, the result is deeper, rounder and more satisfying.

This is the heart of our Samurai Bolognese – Italian tradition, Japanese umami, perfected in Asakura.

ITALIAN TRADITION. JAPANESE UMAMI. ONE UNFORGETTABLE BOLOGNESE.

The Land

Asakura is surrounded by mountains, farmland and rich local food traditions. At PANCETTA, we use local ingredients whenever possible, including Asakura green onions and regional produce, combining them with Italian cooking techniques.
